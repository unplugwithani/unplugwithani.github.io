Why this exists

I found out late
that my sensitivity
was the gift.

I spent years managing it. Dimming it. Performing a version of myself that was easier for everyone else to hold. And all the while, the thing I was managing - the sensitivity, the depth, the feeling of too much - was exactly what I had to offer.

I believe every creative person has a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to leave a unique imprint on the world. And that does not necessarily mean a Netflix deal or a bestselling book. It could mean being a homemaker who makes her home feel like a sanctuary. It could mean being the teacher who impacts the one child who feels inadequate. The person who decides to show up fully on an ordinary day - because if you are going to sweep the kitchen floor, sweep it like it is a palace.

But we get in the way. We think: what is the point? No one is looking. There is too much competition. There is no room for someone like me.

30 Days to Unstuck is my answer to all of that.
