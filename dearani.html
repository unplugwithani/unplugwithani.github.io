What is Dear Ani?

Your questions.
Answered honestly.

Once a month I choose a real letter from a paid subscriber and answer it in full — published exclusively for paid subscribers to read.

The questions can be about anything in my territory. The creative life. Inner work and subconscious patterns. Identity. Relationships. The performing self. Wanting more and not knowing how to ask for it. Whatever is pressing on you right now.

Every letter gets read. Selected letters are answered in the column. All others receive a private reply from me directly.

This is not a Q&A with a stranger on the internet. This is a letter to someone who does this work — and will write back.
